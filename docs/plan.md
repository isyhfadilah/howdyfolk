# Howdyfolk

Build a global, warm, playful modular illustration builder. Stage A: local Next.js prototype with 3 hair styles, 3 expressions, 2 outfits and 2 poses (72 combinations), color controls, reset, random and SVG/transparent PNG export. Validate visual quality before expanding or selling.

Stage B: Supabase email authentication, account-bound Gumroad licenses, PostgreSQL entitlements and private premium Storage. Verify purchases on server; one purchase per matching-email account. Revalidate after 24 hours; no new access on verification failure. Never bundle premium artwork publicly.

Stage C: validate brand/domain, choose collection and price, configure production email and policies, test checkout and deploy to Vercel. One-time purchase grants a specified collection, not all future collections. No subscriptions, teams, vector point editing or customer AI generation in v1.

Acceptance: inspect all 72 combinations, verify editable SVG and 2048px transparent PNG, keyboard/mobile behavior, authentication, license ownership, refunds and direct premium access denial. Current prototype assets are original code-authored exploratory vectors, not approved production AI artwork. Authentication and payment must not be simulated as live services.
